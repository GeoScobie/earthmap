Privacy policy

EarthMap (earthmap.live) is a live satellite map operated by DisasterDB. This page explains what the site collects and how advertising may use cookies.

What the site does

EarthMap shows geostationary satellite imagery (Sat) from NOAA GOES, EUMETSAT Meteosat, and GEO-KOMPSAT-2A. Meteosat imagery contains modified EUMETSAT data.

Information we collect

When you use the site we may collect:

  • Cookie and similar-technology data, including whether you accepted or rejected the cookie banner
  • Device and browser data, and your IP address
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Pages you open, including country map pages, and how you use the map
  • Measurement data through Google Analytics (GA4)

We use this to run the site, understand traffic, and remember your cookie choice. We do not ask you to create an account to view the map.

Advertising

We may show ads on EarthMap, including ads from Google.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting https://adssettings.google.com/. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at https://www.aboutads.info/.

How Google uses information from sites that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If we add another ad network later, we will name it here and link to its site and opt-out.

This page does not load ads.

Cookies

  • A cookie that stores your accept or reject choice
  • Google Analytics cookies for measurement
  • Google advertising cookies, if ads are enabled and you have not opted out

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The map still loads if you reject optional cookies. Rejecting cookies may limit measurement and personalized ads.

Who we share data with

We use Google for analytics and, when ads are on, for advertising. We do not sell your personal information. We share data with those providers only so they can perform those services, under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner on EarthMap to accept or reject measurement and ads cookies
  • Use https://adssettings.google.com/ and https://www.aboutads.info/ to opt out of personalized ads
  • Use your browser controls to block cookies

    About EarthMap

    Disclaimer

    EarthMap provides wildfire, smoke, and related environmental data for informational and situational awareness only. Conditions change quickly; layers can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Do not use EarthMap as a substitute for official evacuation orders, emergency alerts, or air-quality guidance. Always verify with local authorities and official agencies. EarthMap is not liable for decisions made from this map.

    What EarthMap is

    EarthMap is a global wildfire tracking platform built on an entirely self-hosted map stack. It is powered by FireDB, which enhances NASA FIRMS satellite detections with authoritative agency fire points (USA / Canada / Europe / Australia), plus GeoColor (GOES-East/West, Meteosat, GK2A), North America smoke, GFS wind, burned areas, GDACS, satellite tracks, and Google DeepMind hurricane tracks. Browse incidents on disasterdb.com/firedb.

    The basemap is served from our own infrastructure: Protomaps vector tiles on Cloudflare R2, NASA Blue Marble imagery, and self-hosted Lexend map fonts. No third-party map provider sits between you and the data.

    Data sources

    Agency fire points

    • USA Fire Points — agency status; Fire Weather Index where shown.
    • Canada Fire Points — agency status; fire danger rating where shown.
    • European Fire Points — agency / heat footprints where shown.
    • Australia Bushfire Points — live agency points.

    Fire of Note

    Fire of Note means an agency Fire of Note or, in North America, roughly ~1,000 acres of growth in the last 24 hours. Status legend also includes Out of Control, Being Held / Under Control, and Out.

    Satellite hotspots

    • Hotspot clusters / NASA FIRMS — multi-sensor thermal points (VIIRS, MODIS, Sentinel-3 SLSTR where shown). Newer detections appear stronger; older ones fade.

    Geostationary GeoColor

    • GeoColor — NOAA GOES-East, NOAA GOES-West, EUMETSAT Meteosat, KMA GK2A. Himawari may be parked — do not assume live.

    Wildfire smoke

    • Surface PM2.5 / wildfire smoke — RDAQA (Environment and Climate Change Canada); North America; BETA.

    Wind

    • Current and forecast conditions from NOAA’s global GFS model.

    Burned Areas

    • Burned Areas — historical / burned-area product where shown.

    Hurricane Tracks

    • Source: Google DeepMind cyclone forecast.

    GDACS Alerts

    • https://www.gdacs.org

    Fire weather

    • Fire Weather Index (USA) is based on Copernicus / ECMWF global fire weather forecasts. Canada: fire danger rating.
